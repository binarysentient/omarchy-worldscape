Worldscape Forecast Studies

Worldscape: forecast and polish proposals

This mock of the panel has the proposed changes switched on. Toggle them one at a time, or switch to Current to compare. Click a window (or press 1–4) to open the city focus view, and click a highlighted calendar date to preview that day. The data is a fixed sample from Sunday 11 October, 17:32 in Brampton. The palette follows your light/dark setting, the way the widget follows Omarchy.

What each change does

Forecast strip A · sill

Today plus three days under each window, in place of the H · L line, so it costs one extra line. Rain chance appears only at 30% or more, which keeps dry days quiet.

What's next B · sill + home card

When the next 24 hours bring a change (rain starting, clearing, a hot peak), that line replaces the mood line. When nothing changes, the mood line stays. The home card no longer repeats Brampton's mood.

Forecast on the calendar C · calendar

The next seven dates in the month grid get a small home-weather glyph. Clicking one shows that day in the home card. It uses no extra space and puts the calendar and the weather together.

Labelled day ribbon D · ribbon

Replaces the single letters (V, S, B, L, where two could be ambiguous) with name and time, alternating above and below so they don't collide. Home is the accent dot. Sunrise and sunset ticks show where your day sits.

City focus E · click / 1–4

A large window, a 24-hour temperature and rain chart, and the week. The week shows as range bars on one shared scale, or as panes: seven small windows drawn with each day's weather. The overview stays light because the detail lives here.

Time first G · card order

Each card reads top to bottom as time → scene → weather. The ribbon moves above the cards, so the times and the day band sit together. The window, which shows both time of day and weather, then links the two, and the weather text has its own block below it. Clocks line up across a row, and no single block carries all the text.

Weather: Compact / Expanded F · Customize

This row is already in PRINCIPLES.md but not built yet. Expanded adds the strip and next line. Compact keeps only current conditions under each window. One row covers the whole forecast, so there's no separate Forecast setting.
